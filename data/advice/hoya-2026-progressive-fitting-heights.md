# HOYA 2026 Product & Technology Reference Guide — minimum fitting heights

## Free-form progressive lens availability charts

> "Free Form Progressives — Array® / Array® Wrap / Amplitude® HD3/Mini
> Minimum Fitting Height: 21mm/19mm/17mm/14mm | 21mm/19mm/17mm/14mm | 18mm/14mm"
> — *2026 HOYA Vision Care Product & Technology Reference Guide*, lens
> availability charts

This is a per-corridor-length breakdown within HOYA's Array and Amplitude
product families — a single product name covers multiple minimum fitting
heights depending on which corridor length is ordered, the same pattern
Rodenstock and Zeiss document for their own ranges.

## Application to this project

Another independent confirmation of the 14–21mm range for **fitting
height** specifically (see `rodenstock-frame-fitting-heights.md`,
`zeiss-precision-progressive-fitting-heights.md`). This document does not
give a frame/B-height figure; it corroborates the fitting-height side of
the distinction this project's threshold re-provenance turned on, not the
B-height side.
